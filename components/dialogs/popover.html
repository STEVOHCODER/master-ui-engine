Popover

A small annotation surface that appears on hover or focus and steals nothing. It is the explanation counterpart to #dropdown: a dropdown is a menu you click open to choose something, a popover is context you reach for without leaving the page.

Name: Popover
Category: dialogs
Framework: Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS
Defined in: styles/components.css #popover (+ #feedback for the tooltip, #utilities)
Triggers: hover, focus-within, .is-open for tap and scripted control
Slots: popover-wrap, popover-trigger, popover, popover-title, popover-body, popover-list
Placements: above (default), .popover-below, .popover-start, .popover-end
Dependencies: none; the surface is pure CSS, so it renders before any script runs
License: MIT

Annotation on a term

Hover the trigger, or Tab to it: both paths open the same surface. The gap between trigger and panel is bridged by an invisible part of the panel's own box, so walking the pointer across never closes it on the way in. The two panels below are anchored .popover-start and .popover-end rather than centred: a centred surface needs half its width either side of the trigger, so on a near-edge trigger it hangs past the viewport - and a hidden surface hangs there too, because visibility: hidden still lays it out.

Content, not just a sentence

A popover may hold a list, a link or a small figure. Keep it under about 20rem and one screen tall: the moment it needs scrolling it should have been a dialog.

Placements and the pinned state

Below is the other placement, for a trigger that sits near the top of the viewport. The third sample carries .is-open so the surface stays visible in a static screenshot; an application uses it for tap-to-open on touch, where hover never arrives.

.popover-below, .popover-wrap.is-open
Flip when clipped The arrow swaps to the top edge, and the bridge moves with it so the panel remains reachable. .is-open The same visible state hover and focus produce, so a tap handler needs one class toggle and nothing else.

Popover, tooltip, dropdown

Three surfaces, three jobs. A tooltip is one short label and no interaction. A popover is a titled note that may hold a link. A dropdown is a click-opened menu of choices and belongs to #dropdown with the behaviour in styles/mue.js.

.tooltip-wrap > .tooltip (#feedback)
One line of text, never a link The variant that flips under a tight header Tooltips are non-interactive

Accessibility

  • The trigger is a real <button> and points at the surface with aria-describedby, so the annotation is announced with the control
  • The closed surface is visibility: hidden, so its links are out of the tab order until it opens
  • :focus-within opens it on the keyboard, and the ring sits on the trigger rather than on an invisible panel
  • Escape-out and tap-out are the application's job; the sheet exposes .is-open so the state is one class, not a rewrite
  • Surfaces use --bg-surface, --hairline and --shadow-lg, so they are correct in dark without .invert or a second rule
  • Touch devices get .is-open instead of a hover state they cannot reach
