Responsive behavior

Nothing here counts columns. .feature-grid and .pricing are repeat(auto-fit, minmax(...)), so three tiers become two and then one the moment a column stops fitting, and .grid-auto does the same for quotes and team cards. The hero is the only block with a real layout change, and it is a single min-width: 60rem query. Vertical rhythm is --section-pad-y on top of the scale, so a denser page overrides one variable. At 320px nothing overflows: every text slot is min-width: 0, prices are tabular, and the wide tokens in a headline break rather than push.

Accessibility

  • Sections are real <section> elements with a heading inside, so the document outline stays honest
  • The recommended price tier carries a badge as well as a border, and every check glyph in a feature list is aria-hidden decoration next to real text
  • The FAQ uses the #disclosure pattern: a <button> with aria-expanded and aria-controls, operable by keyboard alone
  • Link-only icons in .team-links each carry an .sr-only name and a 44px hit area
  • Decorative artwork is inline SVG with aria-hidden="true"; a meaningful media slot gets role="img" with a label instead
  • Body copy and its backgrounds hold WCAG AA in light and dark; the inverted band is the one place contrast is deliberately authored against a fixed palette
