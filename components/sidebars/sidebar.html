Responsive behavior

Above 900px the sidebar keeps a fixed width beside the content. Below 900px it becomes full-width and stacks above the content, with its rule moving from the right edge to the bottom — all handled by the #sidebar region, no drawer script.

Accessibility

  • <aside aria-label> landmark
  • Icons are decorative (aria-hidden); labels are real text in .sidebar-label
  • The active link pairs a colour with a tint background, and every cell is 44px tall
