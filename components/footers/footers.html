Responsive behavior

The #footers region lays the blocks in an auto-fit grid at a ~11rem minimum, so the columns drop from four to one as the viewport narrows without a breakpoint. Social icons are 36px tap targets.

Accessibility

  • Semantic <footer> landmark
  • Text colour is token-driven, so contrast holds in both light and dark
  • Focus-visible rings on every link; social icons carry aria-labels
